Effect on the Haiku cost regression

With the earlier JEV estimate, the Evidence Layer arm cost $0.009965 against $0.007754 for Traditional: +$0.002210. Without JEV, system cost becomes $0.006698 against $0.007876: −$0.001178. Removing JEV therefore fixes the cost regression, but completeness falls by 0.225 and gold-passage recall by 0.129: it is not a free win.
