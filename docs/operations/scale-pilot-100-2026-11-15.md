# 100-workspace publication pilot — 2026-11-15

Status: **PASS after a corrected harness upload.**
One owner, 100 workspaces, 100 sources, two verification worker replicas.

## Initial harness issue

The first harness upload used a bodyless request before the real upload, which
created zero-byte Storage objects. `begin_snapshot_verification` correctly
rejected them as having no verifiable staged object, and the worker retried as
`worker_error`. This was a test-harness bug, not a product publication defect.

The harness then:

1. re-uploaded the exact content with the service role;
2. restored `storage.objects.owner_id` to the source creator;
3. requeued the 100 jobs with `attempts=0`.

## Interpretation

Two worker replicas drained 100 jobs in roughly 2.7 minutes (about 37
publications per minute). The earlier controlled two-replica pilot measured an
average job latency of 6.89 seconds. The initial failure exercised the retry
path without publishing bad bytes.

## Residual

- 100 synthetic workspaces and their immutable published sources remain, in
  addition to the earlier 10-workspace and cleanup pilots. They contain
  synthetic content only.
- The worker remains at 2 replicas.
- Cleanup and quotas are active for future data.
- A malformed object upload remains a retryable worker error until the job
  becomes dead; operators should alert on dead cleanup/verification jobs.
